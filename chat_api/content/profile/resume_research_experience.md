# Aaron Chen Research Experience

## Johns Hopkins University — Doctoral Researcher
Period: August 2019 to July 2023
Advisor: Professor Paulette Clancy
Degree outcome: Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering
Thesis: Molecular Simulation toward Energy Applications: From Traditional Models to Machine Learning

Aaron developed active-learning machine-learning force fields and multiscale simulation workflows for semiconductor crystal growth, especially gallium nitride in liquid phase. His work connected atomistic growth energetics, diffusion barriers, and defect formation to experimentally observed thin-film and crystal-growth behavior.

He designed steered molecular-dynamics studies to quantify step-edge barriers and diffusivity, built reproducible data-generation and analysis workflows, and collaborated with experimental and theoretical partners across the United States and Germany. He also contributed technical content to NSF and DOE proposal development.

## Cornell University — Master Researcher
Period: November 2017 to August 2019
Advisor: Professor Paulette Clancy
Degree outcome: M.S. in Chemical Engineering
Thesis: Computational Study of Solvent Effect on Aggregation of Core-Chlorinated Naphthalene Tetracarboxylic Diimide and Its Interaction with Solvent Molecules

Aaron applied enhanced-sampling, free-energy, and force-field methods to study solvent effects in organic semiconductor processing. He parameterized force fields, identified new molecular conformations with density-functional-theory calculations, and extended simulation coverage to organic systems that had not previously been modeled well.

## University of California San Diego — Undergraduate Researcher
Period: September 2014 to June 2017
Advisor: Professor Liangfang Zhang

Aaron supported experimental workflows for nanoparticle-based therapeutics, including in vivo studies, membrane-isolation protocols, and cell-membrane-coated nanoparticle stability studies for inflammatory arthritis, gastric cancer, and H. pylori drug-delivery research.
