# Aaron Chen Resume Summary

Aaron Chen is a machine learning scientist, materials scientist, and computational engineer with more than seven years of experience across industry and academia.

Public technical focus areas:
- machine learning for materials and molecular systems
- atomistic simulation and multiscale modeling
- Bayesian optimization and active learning
- scientific software, data pipelines, and reproducible workflows
- scientific computing on cloud and HPC environments

Aaron's industry work includes drilling analytics, gas-separation membranes, carbon-capture chemistry, polymer simulation, and materials informatics. His academic work includes machine-learning force fields, crystal growth, solvent effects in organic semiconductors, and nanoparticle therapeutics research.
